What We Do

Integrated Security Services

From a single guard for one night to a full security programme for your business, we tailor every service to your site, your risks, and your budget.

Static & Site Guards

Uniformed, professional guards for offices, warehouses, retail and residential sites. A visible deterrent and a calm, trained response when it counts.

Mobile Patrols

Scheduled and random patrols of your premises through the night, with lock-up and unlock services, physical checks, and instant reporting.

Alarm Response & Monitoring

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas of Auckland do you cover?

All of them. From our base in Papatoetoe we cover every area of Auckland: South Auckland, Manukau, Auckland Central and the CBD, East Auckland, West Auckland, the North Shore, and the airport surrounds. Wherever you are in Auckland, we can help — and we can travel to nearby regions for larger jobs.

Are you really available 24/7?

Yes. Our phones are answered around the clock, every day of the year including public holidays. Security incidents don't keep business hours, so neither do we.

What types of security do you provide?

Static and site guards, mobile patrols, alarm response and monitoring, event security, construction site security, and retail loss prevention. If you're not sure what your site needs, call us and we'll recommend the right level of cover.

How quickly can a guard be on site?

That depends on the cover you need, but we regularly arrange short-notice and urgent cover. Call 0210 570 343 and we'll tell you straight away what's possible for your timeframe.

Do you take one-off jobs, or only ongoing contracts?

Both. We cover everything from a single guard for one night to a full ongoing security programme for your business, tailored to your site, your risks, and your budget.

How much does security cost?

Every site is different, so we quote based on your site, hours, and the level of risk. Quotes are free, no-obligation, and we keep our pricing clear with no overselling.
